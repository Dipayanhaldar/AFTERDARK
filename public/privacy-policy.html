AFTERDARK · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AFTERDARK handles information when you use the AFTERDARK application, invitation links, guest verification and private chat features. AFTERDARK is an invitation-based communication service operated by its developer.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, AFTERDARK may process:

  • Account information: email address, display name, authentication identifiers and session information.
  • Invitation and connection information: invitation tokens, invite status, creator and guest display names, OTP verification records and connection status.
  • Communication content: chat messages and, when you choose to send them, photos, voice messages or related media and message metadata.
  • Technical information: device identifiers, IP/network information supplied with requests, app version, timestamps and basic operational or error data.
  • Optional sign-in information: information made available by a third-party identity provider when you choose its sign-in option.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and manage accounts, generate and validate invitations, verify guest access using OTPs, establish and operate one-to-one conversations, deliver messages and media, maintain sessions, protect service availability, troubleshoot faults and respond to support or privacy requests.

3. Invitations, OTPs and chat privacy

Invitation links contain a unique invitation reference and may include display-name hints. A separate OTP is used as part of guest verification. Treat invitation links and OTPs as private and share them only with the person you intend to invite.

AFTERDARK is designed for invitation-based one-to-one communication. This does not mean that every item of information is end-to-end encrypted. Some message encryption features may be available, but users should not assume that all messages, media, backups or metadata are encrypted end-to-end in every configuration.

4. Storage and service providers

Application data may be processed by infrastructure and service providers used to operate AFTERDARK, including hosting, database and authentication providers. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and may store it in data centres in different locations. Their own privacy terms also apply to their services.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to infrastructure providers supporting the app, when required by law or valid legal process, to protect the service and its users, or when necessary to investigate abuse, security incidents or technical failures. Information you choose to share with your invited chat partner is visible to that partner.

6. Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the service, maintain an active account or connection, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes and comply with applicable law. Revoking an invitation disables that invitation; it does not necessarily erase previously exchanged messages or all associated records. You may request deletion of account or associated information through the support contact published on the app's store listing. Some records may remain for a limited period where required for security, legal compliance or backup recovery.

7. Your choices

You can choose whether to create an account, use optional sign-in features, share an invitation, or send particular messages and media. You can stop using the app and revoke an active invitation through the available app controls. You may also request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and necessary verification.

8. Children

AFTERDARK is not designed for children who are below the minimum age permitted to use the service under applicable law. Do not use the service or submit personal information if you are not legally permitted to do so. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer through the support contact on the app's store listing.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to help protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your account credentials, invitation link and OTP confidential, and notify the developer if you suspect unauthorised access.

10. Third-party services and links

The app may rely on third-party services or contain links to external websites. Their collection and use of information are governed by their own policies. Review those policies before using the relevant third-party service.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use, to the extent permitted by law.

12. Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, use the developer support contact displayed on the AFTERDARK app's official store listing. Include “Privacy Request” in the subject and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant account without sending your password or OTP.
