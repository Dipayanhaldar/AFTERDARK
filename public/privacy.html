AFTERDARK · PRIVACY POLICY & 18+ SAFETY COMPLIANCE

AFTERDARK Privacy Policy & Terms of Consent

Package ID: com.afterdark.social  |  Effective Date: November 2026

1. Overview & Mutual Consent Principle

AFTERDARK is a strictly 18+ consent-first, one-to-one private communication and date-planning application for couples and best friends. Connections can only be established when a signed-in host generates a single active invite QR code and 6-digit passcode, and the invited partner explicitly confirms 18+ mutual consent and enters the passcode.

2. Data We Collect & How It Is Used

  • Account Identity: Display name/nickname and email address (when creating a host account via email or Google Sign-In) solely to authenticate your session.
  • One-to-One Session Data: Invite tokens, 6-digit passcodes, and private 1-on-1 messages/media shared within your approved room.
  • Optional Device Permissions: Camera (`CAMERA`) and Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) permissions are requested strictly on demand when you choose to send a live photo snap or voice note in chat. Notification (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) permission is used to alert you when your partner sends a message.

3. Data Retention, Revocation & Account Deletion

  • Instant Revocation: Clicking "Revoke Access & Remove QR Code" immediately terminates the active invite link, invalidates the 6-digit passcode, and closes the guest session.
  • Automatic Expiration: Invite links automatically expire after 7 days, and chat records are configured for automatic 30-day TTL cleanup.
  • Zero Third-Party Data Selling: AFTERDARK never sells personal data or private chat contents to third-party advertisers. Contextual brand suggestions and Flipkart/commerce links open only when tapped by the user.

4. Compliance (DPDP Act India, Samsung Galaxy Store & PhonePe Indus Appstore)

AFTERDARK complies with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, Samsung Galaxy Store Seller Portal guidelines, and PhonePe Indus Appstore developer policies. Users may delete their account or revoke active sessions at any time directly inside the app.
